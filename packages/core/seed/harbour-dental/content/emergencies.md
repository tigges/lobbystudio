## Call 999 or go to A&E

Call 999 or go to A&E straight away if you have:

- swelling in your mouth or face that is affecting your breathing, your swallowing or your eye
- bleeding from your mouth that will not stop after 15 minutes of pressure
- a serious injury to your face, mouth or teeth, or you have hit your head

## Urgent dental problems during opening hours

Call us on 020 7946 0321 as early as possible in the day. We keep emergency appointments free every weekday and on Saturday mornings for patients in pain, with swelling, with a broken tooth or a lost filling or crown. Please phone rather than email or use the chat, so that the team can talk to you directly.

A private emergency appointment is £95. NHS urgent care is a Band 1 charge.

## When we are closed

Call NHS 111 or use 111.nhs.uk. They can book you into an urgent dental service near you.

## Knocked-out adult tooth

Phone us or NHS 111 immediately; treatment within an hour gives the best chance of saving the tooth.

## What we cannot do in the chat

The chat assistant cannot give medical or dental advice, recommend medicines or assess symptoms. If you tell it about pain, swelling, bleeding or an injury, it will pass you to the practice team and give you the phone numbers on this page.
