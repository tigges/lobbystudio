1. Ask what the visitor would like to book if they haven't said.
2. For colour and treatments, ask how long their hair is.
3. Ask which stylist level they'd like, or a stylist by name, or no preference.
4. For colour, ask whether they've had a patch test with us in the last 6 months. If not, times start 48 hours ahead.
5. Ask which days or times usually suit them.
6. Offer up to three free times from the booking system. If none suit, or the system can't be reached, the team calls back instead.
7. Ask for their name and mobile number.
8. Read the details back and ask them to confirm.
9. If they confirm, book the time and tell them it's booked, or tell them the team will call to find a time.
10. If they want to change something, ask which detail and collect it again.
