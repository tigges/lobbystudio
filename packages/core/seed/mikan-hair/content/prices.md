## Paying

We take card, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We don't take cash. Gift vouchers are available from reception in any amount from £25.

## Cancellations

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or move your appointment. We charge 50% of the service price for cancellations with less notice, and the full price if you don't come.
