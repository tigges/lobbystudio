## Patch tests before colour

We need a patch test at least 48 hours before any colour service, including root colour, toners, highlights and balayage. This is required by our insurers and by the colour manufacturers, and we can't colour your hair without one.

- A patch test takes five minutes and is free.
- You can walk in for one during opening hours; there's no need to book.
- If you've had a patch test with us in the last 6 months, you don't need a new one unless you've had a reaction to anything since.
- Please don't wash the area for 48 hours afterwards.

We also need a new patch test if you've had a temporary black henna tattoo since your last one, or if you've started new medication.

## If you have a reaction

If the skin around a patch test, or your scalp after a colour, becomes itchy, red, sore or swollen, wash the area with cool water and call us on 020 7946 0588. Please don't wait for your appointment. We will not colour your hair until we know what happened.

For medical advice, contact NHS 111 or speak to a pharmacist.

## Call 999 if you have a severe reaction

Call 999 straight away if, after a patch test or a colour, you have:

- swelling of your face, lips, tongue or throat
- difficulty breathing or swallowing
- a widespread rash with feeling faint or unwell

## Aftercare

- Wait 48 hours before washing your hair after colour, so the colour can settle.
- Use a sulphate-free shampoo to make colour last longer.
- Book a toner or gloss every 6 to 8 weeks to keep blondes bright.

## What we can't advise on in the chat

The chat assistant can't give medical advice or tell you whether a treatment is safe for you, for example during pregnancy, with a scalp condition or with medication. If you ask about a reaction, a scalp problem or your health, it will pass you to the salon team, who can talk to you directly.
