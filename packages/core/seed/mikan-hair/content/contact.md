Mikan Hair Studio is a small Japanese-run salon off Turnham Green Terrace in Chiswick, west London. The studio started in 2016 and specialises in precision cutting, soft lived-in colour and Japanese hair treatments.

## Getting here

Our nearest station is Turnham Green (District line), a three-minute walk away. The E3, 27 and 94 buses stop on Chiswick High Road. There is pay-and-display parking on the surrounding streets but no salon parking. The salon is on the ground floor with step-free access; our basins and toilet are on the same floor.
