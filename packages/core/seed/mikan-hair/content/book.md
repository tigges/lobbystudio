## How to book

- **In the chat:** choose "Book an appointment". The assistant asks what you'd like, how long your hair is and who you'd like to see, then shows you the next free times and books you straight into our diary. If none of the times suit, the team will call you back to find one.
- **By phone:** call 020 7946 0588 during opening hours.

We don't take bookings by email or Instagram.

## Before your colour appointment

Come in for a patch test at least 48 hours before. It takes five minutes and you can just walk in.

## Changing or cancelling

Call us on 020 7946 0588 at least 24 hours before to move or cancel your appointment. We send a text reminder two days before.

## Arriving

Please arrive five minutes early. If you're more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten your appointment or move it.
