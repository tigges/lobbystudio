## Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when a stylist is free. Walk-ins are most likely on Tuesday to Thursday mornings. Saturdays are usually fully booked a week ahead, so we recommend booking.

## How long will my appointment take?

A cut and blow-dry takes about an hour. A full head colour takes about two hours, and balayage or full head foils about three hours. Your booking confirmation shows the time we've set aside.

## Which stylist level should I book?

Graduate stylists have finished our two-year training programme and are great for cuts and blow-dries at a lower price. Stylists and senior stylists have more experience with complex cuts and colour. Our creative director, Aiko, specialises in restyles and precision bobs. If you're not sure, choose "No preference" and we'll match you with whoever's free.

## Do you do consultations?

Yes, free 15-minute consultations for colour changes, colour correction, restyles and smoothing treatments. You can have your patch test at the same time.

## Do you cut curly or textured hair?

Yes. Hana and Sam specialise in curly and textured hair and cut it dry first to see your natural shape.

## Do you do hair extensions or nails?

No. We don't offer hair extensions, wigs, nails, brows or beauty treatments; we focus on cutting, colour and hair treatments.

## Do you cut children's hair?

Yes, for children under 12 at our children's price. Please come with them for the appointment.

## Can I bring my dog?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome on a lead, as long as other clients are happy.

## Is there wifi?

Yes, ask at reception for the password. We also have tea, Japanese green tea and coffee.
