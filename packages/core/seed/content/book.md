## How to book

- **Booking request:** use the chat on this site and choose "Book a check-up". The assistant will take your name, phone number and preferred times, and the team will call you back to confirm, usually the same working day.
- **By phone:** call 020 7946 0321 during opening hours.

We do not take bookings by email.

## Your first visit

Please arrive 10 minutes early. Bring a list of any medicines you take and, if you are claiming free NHS treatment, proof of your exemption. A new patient examination takes 45 minutes and includes two small x-rays if your dentist needs them.

## Evening and Saturday appointments

Evening appointments (Tuesday and Thursday until 8pm) and Saturday appointments (9am to 1pm) are for private patients and Harbour Care plan members.

## Appointment reminders

We send a text reminder two days before your appointment. Reply C to cancel.
