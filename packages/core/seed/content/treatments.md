## NHS charges

NHS dental charges are set by the government and are the same at every NHS practice in England. The charges from 1 April 2025 are:

| NHS band | What it covers | Charge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Band 1 | Examination, diagnosis, x-rays, scale and polish if clinically needed, and urgent care | £27.40 |
| Band 2 | Everything in Band 1 plus fillings, root canal treatment and extractions | £75.30 |
| Band 3 | Everything in Bands 1 and 2 plus crowns, dentures and bridges | £326.70 |

NHS treatment is free for under-18s, pregnant women and people who have had a baby in the last 12 months, and people receiving certain benefits.

## Paying for treatment

We take card and contactless payments. Private treatment over £1,000 can be spread over 12 months at 0% interest, subject to approval. We do not take cash.
