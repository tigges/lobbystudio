## Are you taking new NHS patients?

We are accepting new NHS patients under 18. For adults, we have an NHS waiting list; the current wait to join is around four months. You can ask to join the waiting list by phone or with a booking request. Adults can register as private patients straight away.

## What is the difference between NHS and private treatment?

NHS treatment covers everything that is clinically necessary to keep your mouth, teeth and gums healthy, at the national NHS charges. Private treatment gives you longer appointments, more choice of materials (for example white fillings on back teeth), cosmetic treatments and evening and Saturday appointments.

## Can I have a mix of NHS and private treatment?

Yes. Your dentist will explain which parts of your treatment plan are NHS and which are private before you agree to anything.

## Do I need to be registered with you?

There is no formal registration for NHS dentistry in England. You become our patient once you have a check-up with us. If you have not visited for two years, we may treat you as a new patient.

## Do you see children?

Yes. Children under 18 are seen on the NHS at no charge. We recommend a first visit by your child's first birthday.

## Do you offer a dental plan?

Yes. Our Harbour Care plan is £14.50 a month for adults and includes two check-ups and two hygienist appointments a year, plus 10% off other private treatment. There is no plan for NHS patients.

## How do I cancel or change an appointment?

Please give us at least 48 hours' notice by phone. Private appointments cancelled with less than 48 hours' notice may be charged a £30 fee. Missing two NHS appointments in a row may mean we cannot offer further NHS appointments.

## Do you offer sedation?

We do not offer sedation at the practice. If you are a nervous patient, tell us when you book and we will give you a longer first appointment. If you need sedation, we can refer you.
