## Getting here

We are a four-minute walk from Canada Water station (Jubilee line and Overground). The C10 and 47 buses stop on Quayside Row. There is no on-site parking; the nearest public car park is at Canada Water. The practice is on the ground floor with step-free access and an accessible toilet.
